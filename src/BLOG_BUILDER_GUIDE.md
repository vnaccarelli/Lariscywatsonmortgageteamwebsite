## 📝 Blog Post Builder - Quick Start Guide

### Accessing the Blog Builder
Navigate to: `#/blog-admin-secret` in your browser's address bar

Example: `https://your-site.com#/blog-admin-secret`

### Features

1. **Blog Post Manager** - View all your blog posts (drafts & published)
2. **Rich Text Editor** - Write and format your blog content
3. **Image Upload** - Add featured images via URL or file upload
4. **Profile Settings** - Set your author name, bio, and avatar
5. **Draft/Publish** - Save drafts or publish immediately

### Important Notes

⚠️ **This is a localStorage demo version**
- Data is stored in your browser only
- Clearing browser data will delete all posts
- Images uploaded from your computer are stored as base64
- Not suitable for production use

🔄 **Migration Required**
When ready for production, we'll migrate to Supabase for:
- Proper database storage
- Real image uploads
- Cross-device access
- Team collaboration

### Workflow

1. **Set up your profile** (Settings button)
2. **Create a new post** (New Post button)
3. **Add a title** and content
4. **Upload an image** (URL or file)
5. **Choose category** and read time
6. **Save as draft** or **publish**

### Tips

- Use meaningful titles for better SEO
- Add excerpts to summarize your post
- Choose appropriate categories
- Preview before publishing
- Draft posts are not visible to visitors
